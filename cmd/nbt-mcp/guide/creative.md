# Creative style

A creative-style build is a showpiece: the builder had unlimited blocks and time. It should look intentional at every scale, from silhouette to the single button.

## Materials

- Any block is fair game, chosen for colour and texture rather than cost. Use the full families: all the stair, slab and wall variants of a material, plus its polished, chiselled, cracked and mossy forms.
- Mix textures within one role. A stone wall might be stone_bricks, cracked_stone_bricks, mossy_stone_bricks, andesite, tuff_bricks and a touch of cobblestone. A roof might blend deepslate_tiles, deepslate_bricks and cobbled_deepslate stairs.
- Use colour gradients across a surface: dark at the base fading lighter, or warm at the centre and cooler at the edges. Blocks with similar colour but different texture (stone, andesite, tuff, light_gray_concrete_powder) make smooth gradients.
- Accents can be rich: copper (oxidized for age, waxed so it stays), chiselled blocks over doors, banners, stained glass, decorated pots, candles.

## Palette size and detail

- 8 to 15 distinct block types on the exterior, each role with 2 to 4 variants.
- Detail blocks 20 to 35% of the visible surface. Every edge has a treatment: stairs or slabs bevel the tops of walls, trapdoors frame windows, walls and fences become pillars and railings, buttons become rivets.
- Depth everywhere: 3 or more layers on each facade (frame, wall, recessed windows, protruding sills and balconies). No flat run longer than about 5 blocks.

## Scale and layout

- Larger and more complex: multiple volumes, towers, bridges, arches, curved walls (use fill_cylinder and fill_ellipsoid), steep layered roofs with dormers.
- Asymmetry is deliberate: a main symmetric facade with an off-centre tower, extension or overgrowth.
- Landscaping is part of the build: terraced terrain, paths, walls, gardens, trees, water features, rocks of mixed stone.
- Interiors are fully furnished and themed with custom furniture made of stairs, slabs, trapdoors, banners, and item frames.
